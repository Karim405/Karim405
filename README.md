## 👨‍💻 About Me

I’m a **Junior Data Analyst** passionate about transforming raw data into meaningful insights, clean dashboards, and data-driven business recommendations.

I work with **Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI, Pandas, and NumPy** to clean, analyze, visualize, and interpret data.  
My goal is not only to create charts or reports, but to understand the real business problem behind the data and turn it into clear, useful, and actionable insights.

I enjoy working on real-world datasets, building dashboards, writing analytical queries, and presenting data in a simple and professional way.
